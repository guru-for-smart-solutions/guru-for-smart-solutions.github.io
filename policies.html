First: Mr. Josef subscription

  • The free trial lets you get to know the tool before subscribing, and costs nothing.

  • Before work starts: the amount paid is refunded in full.
  • After work has started: we evaluate the work completed up to the date of your request and show you this evaluation in writing. If what you paid is more than the value of the work completed, the difference is returned to you.

Third: how a refund works

  • Request: message us on WhatsApp or by email through the two contact icons fixed on the side of the page, with your name, the email you used to pay and the package or project name.
  • Transfer cost: a small amount, equal to the cost of sending the money back, is deducted from the refund, and we tell you the amount before the refund is completed.
  • Method: the money goes back to the payment method you used, through the same payment gateway. How long it takes to arrive depends on the gateway and the bank that issued your card.
  • If you paid in a currency other than the Egyptian pound, the amount you receive may differ slightly because of exchange rates or your card issuer's fees.
