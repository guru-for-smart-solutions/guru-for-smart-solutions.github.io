First: Mr. Josef subscription

  • The free trial lets you get to know the tool before subscribing, and costs nothing.
  • After a paid subscription you may ask for a refund, provided you have not used any of the credits linked to the subscription.
  • The refund request must be made within 14 days of the payment date.
  • If you have used any part of the credits, a refund is not available for that paid subscription period.
  • You can cancel your subscription at any time to prevent any future renewal; it stays active until the end of the period you paid for.

Second: custom projects (data analytics, websites, automation and others)

  • A project starts with a deposit of 35% of its value, and the rest is paid as agreed in writing between us.
  • Before work starts: the amount paid is refunded in full.
  • After work has started: we evaluate the work completed up to the date of your request and show you this evaluation in writing. If what you paid is more than the value of the work completed, the difference is returned to you.
  • After final delivery and your approval: a refund is not available, but the modifications and support listed in your package still apply within the agreed scope.

Third: how a refund works

  • Request: message us on WhatsApp or by email (shown in the Contact section of the home page) with your name, the email you used to pay and the package or project name.
  • Transfer cost: a small amount, equal to the cost of sending the money back, is deducted from the refund, and we tell you the amount before the refund is completed.
  • Method: the money goes back to the payment method you used, through the same payment gateway (Kashier or XPay). How long it takes to arrive depends on the gateway and the bank that issued your card.
  • If you paid in a currency other than the Egyptian pound, the amount you receive may differ slightly because of exchange rates or your card issuer's fees.
